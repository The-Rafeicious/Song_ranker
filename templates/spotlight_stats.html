How these figures work

Connecting positions run from 0 to 1. Points round half-up from max(0, min(12, 6 × position + 8 × (position − group average))). The 24-point round ceiling is not a target: equal 0%, 50% and 100% positions earn 0, 3 and 6 points per track.

Support uses 3 × points / (12 + 2 × points). Adjusted support moves lightly tested tracks toward 50%. Each completed offer contributes one observation per track. Artist, album and tag support pools those track observations; collaborations and multiple tags can appear in more than one group. Every record list shows at most five entries.

Legacy rounds retain their original fixed 12-point interpretation. Their connection is unknown and excluded from connection averages. Totals, point averages, distribution and history retain saved results for removed tracks; leaderboards and category records use the current catalogue. Three points map to 50% support. Spotlight contributes to Overall's preference family; public Elo stays based on matches.

Activity uses UTC. Skipped rounds are excluded from scoring and retained for seven days. Listening counts playback before saved decisions. History remains paged, with ten rounds per page.
