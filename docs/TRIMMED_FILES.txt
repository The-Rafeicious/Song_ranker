V5.1 is a code-only cumulative upgrade into the supplied V5 layout.

Keep data/song_ranker_v3.db, instance/, database_backups/, .git/ and your local
database_backup.sql. They are intentionally absent from the download.

are main.py and safe_mode.py. Backend modules live under songranker/; see
docs/FILES.md for their current locations. Do not run obsolete root module
copies from a pre-V5 installation as alternative launchers.
