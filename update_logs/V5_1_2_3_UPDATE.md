# V5.1.2.3 — Spotlight reveals and statistics

- Standard result previews last 3.5 seconds per song; Quick uses 1.8 seconds.
  Final settling time is 1.2 / 0.7 seconds, for 15.2 / 7.9 seconds per full pass.
  Manual Next still skips the reveal.
- Spotlight Stats adopts Standard Vote's shared overview, paired inset panels,
  compact metadata rows, activity, coverage, distribution, category records and
  paged history layout, using Spotlight's existing blue-violet accent.
- Show real awarded totals, mean points per round/track, round ranges, zero and
  12-point outcomes, saved connection averages and endpoint counts.
- Separate connecting/legacy round counts. Legacy connection remains unknown;
  removed tracks retain their original contribution to totals and history.
- Track records show five entries each: adjusted support, mean points with 3+
  rounds, appearances, and mean connection with 3+ saved placements.
- Artist, proper-album and tag records also stop at five per list. Category
  support pools track observations; it is labelled separately from adjusted
  track support. No expandable full leaderboard.
- All statistics are read-only projections. Schema and scoring remain unchanged.
